Formula

Flooring cost formula

Flooring cost = floor area x (1 + waste) x material price + underlayment + installation.

Floor area is length x width x rooms, or a total square feet you enter. Typical waste is 5% to 15% depending on the layout.

How to use this flooring cost calculator

Start with room dimensions if you are pricing a bedroom, living room, office, hallway, or apartment. Use total square feet if you already measured the area from a floor plan or a contractor quote.

DIY vs professional installation

For a DIY job, uncheck installation and focus on material and underlayment. For a professional estimate, enter a local install rate per square foot and keep the box checked.

What affects flooring cost?

Flooring cost changes with material type, room size, waste factor, underlayment, and whether you hire a pro. Laminate and vinyl are the budget choices; hardwood and tile cost more but tend to last longer.

Flooring calculator FAQ

How much does flooring cost per square foot installed?

Installed flooring usually runs about $3 to $15 per square foot. Laminate and vinyl land near $3 to $7 installed, while hardwood and tile often run $7 to $15 installed depending on region and prep work.

Should I round flooring up?

Yes. Flooring is sold in cartons that cover a fixed square footage, so round your total up to whole cartons. The shopping list above estimates the square feet to buy including waste.

Does the calculator include trim or baseboards?

No. It covers the field of the floor only. Add quarter-round, transitions, and baseboards as separate line items if your project includes them.

Is this a contractor quote?

No. It is a planning estimate. Subfloor prep, removal of old flooring, furniture moving, and regional labor rates can change the final price.
