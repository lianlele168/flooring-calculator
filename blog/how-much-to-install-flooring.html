How Much Does It Cost to Install Flooring?

Installed flooring typically runs about $3 to $15 per square foot in 2026. A 200 sq ft bedroom usually lands near $600 to $1,200 for laminate or vinyl and $1,500 to $3,000 for hardwood or tile. The numbers below show exactly where that comes from, with a calculator you can tune to your own room.

What changes the price

  • Material: laminate and vinyl are the budget picks; hardwood and tile cost more but last longer.
  • Subfloor prep: leveling compounds, patches, or plywood add labor a per-sq-ft estimate does not capture.
  • Removal: tearing out old carpet, tile, or glued-down flooring is often billed separately.
  • Region: labor in large metros runs higher than rural rates.
  • Pattern: diagonal layouts, herringbone, and lots of cuts raise the waste factor and time.

FAQ

Is it cheaper to install flooring myself?

Yes for material and underlayment only. A DIY 200 sq ft laminate room can cost $300 to $600 in materials, while hiring a pro is usually $600 to $1,200 for the same space. Tile and hardwood are much harder to DIY well.

Why is tile and hardwood so much more?

Both need more skilled labor and more prep. Tile adds mortar, grout, and a longer install; hardwood needs acclimation, nailing or gluing, and often sanding and finishing.

Does this include underlayment?

The base estimate above is field material and install. Add underlayment (about $0.30 to $1 per sq ft depending on type) unless your product has padding attached.
